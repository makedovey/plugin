# The tools this plugin's skills name

Generated by api/scripts/build_plugin_skill.py from the tools' own descriptions (api/core, the registry the MCP server lists). The full list is what your host shows for the `dovey` server.

## add_frame

Add a frame to a workflow: type asset, reference, research, brief, output, text, shots (Product shots, with the designed ads), template or brand (a template canvas), with its data. With after set to a frame id it is placed to the right of that frame and connected to it; without, to the right of everything. Build in order asset → research → brief → output, passing after each time. A new frame starts stale (not run yet).

## confirm_product_cutout

The person confirmed that a product's cutout is their product and looks right: stored on the cutout with who and when. Only after the person said so.

## create_canvas

Start a new canvas (a project with one page). With no seed it is empty, named "Untitled canvas" unless a name is given: build it with add_frame on its workflow_id. With reference_ad_id (an ad id from search_ads) it is the Remix canvas, named "{brand} remix": Reference (that ad) → Asset (empty) → Output (3 ads), connected. Its Run waits for the product photo: update_frame on the Asset with an asset_id (upload_asset or the brand's products), or asset_ids for several photos (each ad uses the next one), then run_workflow when the person says go. Free; nothing runs.

## create_upload_link

Mint a link where the user can upload images from their device straight into this workspace. Use it when the user has a file on hand (chat attachments cannot reach Dovey directly): give them the upload_url to click, they drop their images on that page, and each one lands as an asset here — check list_assets afterwards. The link works for an hour and only adds assets to this workspace. kind defaults to product.

## get_brand_facts

The brand's real numbers and claims (onboarding's "Real numbers and claims"): the lines the person confirmed, else the ones the site read found (its stated stats and proof points, word for word). Once confirmed, ads take numbers and claims only from these lines.

## get_brand_style

The brand's style as its ads use it (onboarding's "This is your brand style"): the headline and label faces, the colours (primary, secondary, accent, background, ink), the button (colour, shape, case, arrow), the logo and its light version, what the site read found, whether the site gave little (then three starter styles), the fonts to choose from, whether the person confirmed it, and a sample ad (a canvas document) drawn in it with their product.

## get_canvas_guide

How to design a good ad on Dovey's canvas, for any agent: the method (brief first, a proven layout before free composition, one section at a time, a screenshot and a verdict after each, fix in place, never start over), the checklist the lint measures, then the HTML contract, type, spacing, the brand rule and the tools. Read it once per session, before the first frame. method=true adds the same steps and checklist as data (the text already has them).

## get_canvas_items

What is on a canvas (the board: frames, groups of frames, text notes, images), as an outline: one line an item with its kind, title, id, box [x,y wxh], a frame's size, design_id and status (done, queued, making, waiting, failed), a group's layout and progress. Read it before changing a canvas and address items by id; a frame's design_id works with every ad tool (get_frame_tree, update_styles, set_text …). Also the canvas's template (a group, a note and the prompt) and the makes running. if_changed_since=rev answers {unchanged} when nothing moved.

## get_job

Status of a background job: queued | running | done | failed. done carries output, failed carries error. While running, stage is a short human line describing the current step, and progress {done, total} counts finished variants on generate jobs. queue_position appears while queued; estimated_seconds is the typical total duration for this kind of job — poll roughly every estimated_seconds/4.

## get_me

Who am I: user, workspace, brand summary, and credit balance. Read-only: call it to verify the connection. setup says what the brand setup has done (brand: the website was read; product: the main product's cutout confirmed; style: the brand style confirmed; facts: the numbers and claims confirmed); when any is false, finish it with the person before the first ad.

## get_onboarding_product

The onboarding's product step: the brand's main product with its cutout (a transparent PNG of the product, url, grade, whether the edges look rough and the one line to show then), the other photos it could be cut from (candidates) and the brand colour it sits on. Whether the person confirmed it is cutout.confirmed.

## get_plan

A plan with its rows, each row's creative (render_url, ad copy) once made, and the person's verdicts.

## get_workflow

A workflow: the document (frames, edges, notes) plus, under hydrated by frame id, what every frame points at: the research ads with their pictures, the brief's rows, the output's creatives (render_url, ad copy, run), the asset's picture. Also which frames are stale (out of date) and what running them would cost. Read this before editing a page.

## list_canvases

The workspace's canvases. Each canvas is one project with one page: a workflow of frames (Reference, Asset, Research, Brief, Output) from research to finished ads. Each with its name, when it was last edited, how many ads it holds (or Draft), its workflow_id for the workflow tools (get_workflow, add_frame, run_workflow), and a picture of its flow. q filters by name; sort is edited (default), name or created.

## make_design

Make an ad creative from words, in the brand: one picture with the headline, a body line and a button in it, the brand's product, logo, colours and type, plus the primary text and headline to publish it with. Pass prompt (the brief in the person's words), product (one of the brand's products by name) and photo_url (a photo of their product they uploaded) when given. For several variations call it once per variation, each with its own brief (a different angle, scene, composition and headline direction), in the same step. Returns the design with its picture.

## make_frames

Make ads on the canvas for the brand: a new group of n frames (one loose frame when n is 1), every frame titled at once and filled in as each ad lands. The batch path: with plan_id (the Brief you wrote with plan_creatives) its rows are the ads; without, the Brief is written first from the prompt. Every ad is made with the designed engine (layouts in the brand's look, no image model) and spread across angles, layouts and grounds, unless the person asked for pictures (engine picture). from_item remakes an image on the canvas (a competitor's ad or an uploaded ad) n times for the brand. group_id makes the frames a make left waiting (the credits ran out) or failed. Runs at once, no confirmation: the credits (1 an ad in Design, 3 in Picture) are reserved now; what the balance does not cover waits. Returns the group and the job.

## make_plan

Make the plan's ads: every row becomes a creative in the brand's colours and type, on the plan's page, three at a time. Returns at once with a job; the card fills in as they land, or poll get_plan. Pass rows to make only some (their n). 3 credits an ad (1 for a plan written with engine design; in mix a layout ad is 1 and the other 2 are given back), reserved now and given back for any ad that fails.

## meta_ad_results

Where these ads stand on Meta (publishing, paused, active, rejected, couldn't publish) and, for ads on Meta, the last 7 days from Meta: spend, impressions, clicks, CTR and cost per result. Read from Meta at most once an hour per ad.

## meta_publish_prepare

Prepare publishing ads to the connected Meta ad account, for the person to confirm. Use this when the person says go, publish or put these live: it never publishes. It returns the settings in plain words (account, objective, daily budget, audience, placements, each ad) and a confirmation the person presses: a card with a Publish button where the assistant shows cards, else confirm_url to open in Dovey. Tell the person what will happen and that they press Publish. go_live=true (the default) is Publish: live at once, spending at the shown budget once they press it; false is Draft: the ads are created paused and spend nothing until the person presses Publish on them.

## plan_creatives

The test plan before a batch: research the category and competitors (or take a research report you already have), then write a table of N ads for this brand, every row different in angle, visual or hook, each with the ad it borrows from and what it tests. The plan lands on a new page of the canvas. Show it as a table; make_plan makes the rows when the person says go (or at once when they asked to make them). A direct simple ask never needs a plan. 1 credit for the research.

## preview_brand_style

The sample ad drawn with these style fields, nothing saved: for showing a change before it is kept.

## refine_design

Change a design by instruction. mode 'edit' (default) changes what the instruction names and keeps the rest ('make it lime', 'move the date to Friday'). mode 'redesign' keeps the words and rebuilds the look: new layout, palette, and an illustration drawn from the content ('make it feel Peruvian', 'more visual', 'professional but colorful'). Returns the design plus a render job.

## remix_ad

Make this ad for the caller's own brand: the same picture and composition with the caller's product in place of theirs, no logos or marks, other people, and every line of copy rewritten in the brand's voice from the brand document. Returns the new design with its picture (render_url) and the primary text and headline to publish it with (ad_copy). Records a remix signal.

## remix_picture

Remix an ad the person uploaded (a screenshot or saved image of another brand's ad) into one for their brand: same composition and idea, their product, words, colours and type. Pass the picture's asset id (in the chat, the attachment's id). Same options and price as remix_ad (3 credits). Returns the creative with its picture and copy.

## research_ads

The research report before making ads: the top performing ads for a category and for named competitors, ranked by Dovey's score (how long an ad ran, versions kept, reach), each with its picture, angle, hook and why it works; the angles and hooks that score best. Static image ads only. Pass competitors as names, websites or Meta page ids (up to six; an account Dovey has not read is read from the Ad Library on the spot, and with a category given only their ads about it are kept). The category itself is asked of the Ad Library when Dovey has few ads for it. 1 credit plus the reads. Shown as a card in Claude, 12 ads at a time.

## rewrite_copy

Change the words that run with a creative (its primary text and headline) without making a new picture. Pass headline and/or primary_text to set them exactly, or instruction ('shorter', 'lead with the price', 'more playful') to have them rewritten. Free. The words inside the picture are changed with refine_design instead.

## run_workflow

Run the workflow: every stale frame in order (research, then brief, then output), or with only=[frame ids] those frames plus anything stale before them. Research is 1 credit, the brief is free, the output is 3 credits an ad (1 an ad when its engine is design; mix reserves 3 and gives 2 back for each layout ad); the credits are reserved now and given back for any ad that fails. Returns the run and the job: poll get_run or get_job. A run already going for this workflow is returned instead of starting a second. Only when the person asked to run or make.

## save_brand_facts

Save the brand's numbers and claims as the person left them (each line real, word for word theirs or the site's; never write one for them). From then on the ad writer uses only these for numbers and claims; an empty list means none. skip=true leaves them unconfirmed and the ads keep to real benefits with no numbers invented.

## save_brand_style

Save the brand's style: the fields given go onto the workspace's design system, which every designed ad, the Template canvases, the Brand frame and the picture engine read. confirm=true (the default) records that the person confirmed it, with who and when; only after the person said it looks right.

## scrape_brand

Scrape a website to (re)build the brand kit: colors, logo, about, voice. Replaces the scraped fields, keeps manual assets. Returns a job to poll with get_job.

## set_product_photo

Give one of the brand's products its photo: the asset id of a picture the person uploaded (in the chat, it is the attachment's id). The photo goes on the product catalogue and into every creative made for that product.

## update_frame

Change part of a frame: its data (a research query, the picked ads, a brief's prompt or n, an output's size or engine), its name, or where it sits. Data is merged key by key. A changed input marks the frame and everything after it stale.

## upload_asset

Upload one of the USER'S OWN images: the product that must appear in generated ads exactly as photographed (kind=product), their logo (kind=logo), or other brand imagery (kind=upload). NOT for other brands' ads whose style should be imitated: those are found with search_ads and remixed by id (remix_ad, or create_canvas with reference_ad_id). How to send it: check list_assets first (scrape_brand and the web app usually uploaded the product already), or pass url for an image already on the web. IMPORTANT: an image attached in the chat, or a file on the user's device, can NOT be sent through this tool — you do not have its bytes, and inventing data_base64 will fail. For those, call create_upload_link and give the user the link instead. data_base64 is only for API clients that genuinely hold the raw file. Several at once: files, a list of up to 10 {filename, data_base64 | url}, all of the same kind; the result lists the assets made and, per file, any that failed and why.
